Everything in the game, in one place. Millennium ships no art, sound or music files: the world is built from its layouts, characters and cars are assembled from simple shapes, and every sound is synthesized when the game starts. The tables below are generated from the game's own data (cargo run -p shared --example catalog), so they always match the game.

Music (8)

Seamless 8- or 16-bar loops, synthesized at startup by a small sequencer: drum patterns as strings, chords as MIDI notes, electric piano, pads, vibes, Karplus-Strong guitar, saw stabs, pipe clanks and a drone. Tracks cross-fade as you move around.

Models and scenery

All drawn from code at runtime, from the same layout data the server uses for collision.

WhatHow it's made
BuildingsBoxes with a band of windows per floor (lit at night), a parapet, a rooftop AC unit, and a door with an awning facing the nearest street. They fade when they'd hide you.
CharactersBlocky bodies in three builds and several skin tones, with jackets, pants, caps and shades in a 1999 palette. Arms and legs swing as they walk; they bob a little when idle.
Y2K BugsGlossy black beetles with a glowing green circuit stripe, red eyes and six legs: the first rivals that aren't people.
AnimalsDogs (ears, a wagging tail, a pale belly), raccoons (a bandit mask, round ears, a ringed tail) and deer (a long neck, antlers, a white tail), on four legs that trot with the walk cycle.
FireworksGlowing rockets that burst into a sphere of sparks (spread on a golden-angle spiral), fading as they fall.
CarsFive shapes: the hatchback, the low turbo coupe, a pickup with a bed, a tall minivan and an open-top roadster. A body, a cabin with windows, and wheels with hubcaps that spin; the body leans into turns. Eight paint jobs, and parts that show: a hood scoop (turbo), chrome hubs (sport tires), a spoiler and side skirts (body kit).
PropsPhone booths, a newsstand, a dumpster, benches, mailboxes, billboards, a basketball hoop, a shipping container, crane legs, oil drums, a scrap heap, a rusted car, gas pumps, the mall's fountain and escalator, a water tower, a pigeon coop, the pirate antenna, pine and oak trees, a lake; a pier into Wren Lake, cabins, semi trailers, a CB tower, a giant fiberglass trucker, server racks and crates; plus the hackable ATMs, arcade cabinets, security panels and terminals, the fishing spot, the CB base station and a filing cabinet.
FurnitureThirteen looks: stove, workbench, desk, bed, footlocker, TV, lamp, poster, beanbag, arcade cabinet, aquarium (with goldfish), boombox and bowling trophy.
StreetsAsphalt with dashed centre lines, and lamp posts every 14 m on alternating sides that light up at night.
Sky and lightA sun that moves with the 40-minute day, a sky that darkens at dusk, and warm streetlamps and windows at night.
InterfaceWindows 2000-style windows: grey bevels, navy gradient title bars, an instant-messenger chat window, classic yellow tooltips.
